# Success Metrics and Measurement Plan

These are proposed metrics. No user test has been run and no outcome improvement is claimed.

## Product usage and usability
- **Task completion rate:** percentage of test users who successfully find open orders for a chosen supplier.
- **Time to identify:** seconds needed to identify an order requiring follow-up.
- **Filter usage:** number/share of test sessions using supplier or status filters.
- **User-reported clarity:** short 1–5 rating after a test task.

## Procurement outcome metrics
- **On-time delivery rate:** delivered orders received on or before the expected date / delivered orders with both dates available.
- **Late delivery count:** delivered orders received after the expected date.
- **Open order count:** orders not marked Delivered.
- **Total order value:** sum of quantity × unit price.

## Measurement plan
Run a small usability test with 3–5 representative users. Record task success and time, note confusion, and compare results after iteration. A small sample is directional, not statistically conclusive.
